The brief

Molarline’s best customers are clinics with great reputations and broken websites. Finding them meant opening hundreds of sites by hand, and template cold emails were going nowhere.

What we built

The brief

HR software sells when hiring outpaces process. That window lasts a few weeks, and static lead lists only show it months later.

What we built

A monitor that reads job boards and careers pages each morning. Bursts of hiring or a first HR job post trigger a scored alert in Slack, and one click sends outreach tied to that moment.

Timing does the selling. The sales team approves every email, so the system moves fast without ever sending something nobody saw.

The brief

Northfold already gets enquiries. They just answer them the next afternoon, by which point the prospect has booked with someone faster.

What we built

A responder that researches every enquiry the moment it arrives, scores it against Northfold’s rules, and replies personally. Good fits get times to book, early ones get a nurture track, poor fits get a kind no.

Why it lands

The prospect gets a thoughtful answer while your site is still open in their browser. The team only steps in for calls worth taking.
